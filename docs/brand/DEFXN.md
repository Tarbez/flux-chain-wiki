# DEFXN / Brand foundation

The block mesh is the brand foundation. Backgrounds, lifecycle illustrations and “How it works” share one cell renderer: `ArkUI.createMeshFabric`. The brand system develops that existing structure rather than introducing a separate ornamental language.

## Color roles

`css/flux-themes.css` is the single source of color. It is generated by `scripts/build-flux-themes.cjs` (edit the families there and rerun `node scripts/build-flux-themes.cjs`). `css/themes.css` (SDK-generated) still loads first for fonts and sizes, but every color token it declares is redeclared in `flux-themes.css`. The live reference is the `/palette` page: switch themes in the settings panel to compare families.

| Role | Token | Where it may appear |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | `--primary` | Identity: the hero accent and a few small brand marks. Never a button, a heading color, or a hover/selected state |
| Secondary | `--secondary` (= `--action`, `--complement`) | The main call to action, and nothing else |
| Reference | `--reference` | Secondary actions, links, and every hover/selected/current state |
| Success / Warning / Danger | `--success`, `--warning`, `--danger` | State only |

Everything else (headings, record numbers, labels, captions, rules) is neutral, so the few colored things keep their weight.

### Harmony

Palettes are built in OKLCH, a perceptual color space, by three rules:

1. Each family states a hue relationship between its three roles (analogous, split-complementary, complementary, or earth analogous).
2. The three roles share a balanced lightness band. Accents now target vivid chroma (0.16–0.17 before gamut mapping), with readable inks and semantic shades; the homepage uses identity color for its highlighted history and secondary color for its CTA and selected audience.
3. Canvas, surfaces and text are near-neutral (0.002 chroma). Color belongs to the chain and actions, not a brown, purple or green cast across the page.

State hues are chosen per family, inside fixed ranges, to sit as far as possible from that family's roles.

### Shades

Every role has semantic shades, and every tint on the site is one of them (no ad-hoc opacity on a role color):

| Step | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `-100` | Subtle fill: soft and quiet buttons, selected states |
| `-200` | Hover fill |
| `-300` | Border |
| `-400` | Strong border, rules, inactive marks |
| `-500` | Base, the same as the bare token |
| `-600` | Hover on a filled button |
| `-700` | Text on a subtle (`-100`) fill |
| `-ink` | Text on a `-500` fill |

Primary, secondary and reference also have an 11-step tonal scale (`--{role}-tone-50` … `-950`) for illustration.

### Components

`css/components.css` loads last and is the one place interactive color is decided; page stylesheets own layout and type only.

| Variant | Paint | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solid | secondary `-500`, `-600` hover, `-ink` text | The main CTA, once per view |
| Soft | secondary `-100` fill, `-300` border, `-700` text | A repeated main action (Run DEFXN in the header) |
| Quiet | reference `-100` fill, `-300` border, `-700` text | Secondary actions and utilities |
| Choice | neutral at rest; reference `-100` / `-400` / `-700` when selected | Tabs, pickers, toggles, current page |
| Link | reference text, `-300` underline, `-700` hover | Text links, and actions that sit in a row of links |

Focus is always a 2px reference ring.

### Home: colour as data

The home page (`css/home-swiss.css`, `js/pages/home-flow.js`) follows five rules, checked by `tests/home-color-semantics.cjs`:

1. **Semiotic utility.** Hue appears only where it answers "what does this do?": the one solid action (secondary), a thin reference mark on the current selection, and the identity colour on the single record whose state the hero steps change (J42), its head marker and the reference its resolution creates. The headline, numbers, histories and checks are monochrome. "Not one chain." is faint grey because it is the rejected idea.
2. **No optical vibration.** An accent only ever touches a neutral: canvas, anchor black, white or grey. Text on an accent uses its `-ink`.
3. **Gravity.** `--anchor` is the heaviest neutral in each appearance (pure black in dark, near-black in light), with `--anchor-ink` for text on it. The hero's measurement strip, the illustration's readout and the closing block sit on it. The small, bright head marker on the right of the hero counterweighs the heavy headline on the left.
4. **Saturation compression.** The page uses neutrals and role bases (`-500`, about 95–100% HSL saturation) only. The `-100`…`-400` shades are the muddy middle and are not used here.
5. **Luminance proportions.** `--{role}-scale` (0.72–1) shrinks an accent's painted area as its relative luminance rises. It is derived in `scripts/build-flux-themes.cjs`, not tuned per theme. It sizes the home CTA, the J42 inlay and the head marker. Dark appearances (bright accents) come out at about 0.8, light ones at about 0.98.

The hero has two drawings. On the left, `ArkUI.buildChainWorld` draws your chain as the heavy line of a transit map, with five other chains crossing it at interchanges. On the right, the landscape puts your chain in the middle of four others, each tied to one of your records by a reference. Each example draws its own counterpart: a chain for a transfer, a pool for a swap, a validator's stake for staking, and a ballot that other chains' votes converge on for governance. Choosing an example highlights the same counterparty on both sides. Icons come only from `js/ark/icons.js` (`ArkUI.icon`, or `ArkUI.iconPaths` laid flat in a drawing), and every icon sits next to a text label. The home page uses the same nav as every other page (no home-only header rules), with the DEFXN mark beside the wordmark.

## Palettes

Five families, each in a dark and a light palette. Hues are OKLCH degrees.

| Family | Dark / light key | Harmony | Primary | Secondary (main CTA) | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ember | `ghost` / `glacier` | Split-complementary | Terracotta (45) | Teal (192) | Slate blue (250) |
| Tide | `clay` / `grove` | Analogous | Sea blue (232) | Periwinkle (282) | Teal (190) |
| Moss | `ochre` / `moss` | Earth analogous | Moss (132) | Clay (46) | Dusty blue (236) |
| Dune | `marine` / `bone` | Complementary | Wheat (66) | Dusty blue (248) | Mauve (318) |
| Dusk | `dusk` / `dawn` | Analogous | Lavender (300) | Rose (350) | Blue (228) |

No two families share a primary hue or a main-CTA hue. Ember dark is the default.

## Backgrounds

Blocks uses the network arrangement, Traces the stepped logic path, and Layers the checking rows, all drawn in the same 16 × 12 cells used by the existing shared page diagrams. Background mode uses neutral ink and omits narrative captions. Patterns animate by default for new preferences; an explicitly saved animation opt-out stays static. Each pattern saves its own size and spacing presets, structure, visibility, animation preference and loop delay. Size and spacing use keyboard-accessible radio groups; Structure changes the arrangement, and Visibility adjusts decorative ink. Remove pattern clears all decorative lattice layers. Pattern-specific 3.2-second waves wait 300 ms–10 seconds between loops (1.2 seconds by default); they stop when hidden, globally paused, or reduced motion is requested. Backgrounds use neutral cell clusters, stepped traces, layered bands and linked nodes without miniature pseudo-data. Cached canvas paths batch the drawing into at most 32 alpha bands per animation frame, capped at 30 paints per second. The background sits below page content, never over buttons or cards. Slider cards pair explanatory labels, percentage/second badges, filled tracks, 44px interaction areas and endpoint descriptions. Native input updates preserve pointer capture and keyboard focus without rerendering. Sliders affect the decorative pattern only; narrative diagrams keep their canonical geometry. Nodes marks linked intersections, Routes draws stepped paths, Tiles groups alternating cell clusters, and Frames outlines repeated block regions. Wave is removed; saved Wave preferences migrate to Traces. None removes the pattern.

Choices persist under `defxn-motif-v1`. Older ornament choices migrate to mesh patterns; the prior weave choice migrates to Wave. Patterns are decorative, excluded from accessibility and fade out through the reading area. Mobile reduces their opacity.

The color regression (`tests/theme-color-contrast.cjs`) checks all ten palettes for: palette authority, secondary owning every action token, perceptually distinct roles and states, balanced role lightness, vivid accents, near-neutral canvases, ordered shade ladders and tonal scales, readable text on fills and on subtle fills, 4.5:1 inks, a four-step text ladder, surface separation, visible borders, and distinct families. `tests/components.cjs` checks that the component layer loads last and is the only place action color appears, that pages use shade tokens rather than ad-hoc tints, and that no interaction state uses the identity color. These are scoped assertions, not a blanket claim about every site element.
