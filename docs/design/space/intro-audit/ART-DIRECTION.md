# Art direction: the twelve ring emblems

Layer: app. Reader: builder A, builder B and the integrator. Written 2026-11-09 against `AUDIT.md`. The owner: "keys need to look futuristic like cards".

## The one job

A stranger names the topic in one glance at 80 to 120 px tall in a 1440x900 frame. One silhouette, not a scene.

## Family rules

1. **One silhouette.** Two to four blocks, one idea. Test: shrink your screenshot to 100 px tall. If the idea is gone, the emblem fails.
2. **One bounding size.** Every emblem fits a box 1.2 m wide, 1.2 m tall, 0.4 m deep, and fills at least 1.0 m in width or height. At the hold camera (15.2 m, 35 mm) that is 86 to 103 px. Emblems are reliefs facing the camera, at least 3:1 wide to deep.
3. **Proportions.** Slabs at 1.6:1 (the card ratio). Thickness one twentieth of the long side. Gaps between parts one tenth of the long side, so parts separate at 100 px.
4. **Three materials, in this share.** `p1stone` matte dark body: 75 percent or more of the lit area. `p1mint`: 15 percent at most, as straight strips 0.02 to 0.04 m wide, flush or 5 mm proud. `p1amberdim`: one accent per emblem, 5 percent at most, only where it means "live" or "needs attention". No white, no emissive outlines, no baked textures, no glass.
5. **One light.** Key from high left on the camera's side, as on the FXN ID card. Stone faces read mid-dark. Left and top edges catch one thin bright line. No lamp inside the emblem.
6. **Still at rest.** Motion belongs to the hover and the film.
7. **Facts only.** No coins, no payout, no crowd of nodes. `00-facts-genesis.md` holds what is true.

## How a card reads

The FXN ID card is the family head. A card is a thin slab at 1.6:1 in `p1stone`. Two corners on one short side are chamfered, 1/12 of the short side each. One `p1mint` strip runs the full length of one long edge, inset by its own width. One chip (a raised square, 1/5 of the short side) sits at one third along the long side, or one notch of that size is cut into the chamfered end. The card faces the camera, twisted 10 degrees at most.

A key is a card. A record is a card. A release is a card with a seal.

## Avoid

Wire outlines. Padlocks, old bit keys, shields, gems, coins. Any copy of the DEFXN mark. Rings or discs, which read as the portal itself. Glow, halos, fog, blur. Parts under 0.1 m. Two ideas in one emblem.
