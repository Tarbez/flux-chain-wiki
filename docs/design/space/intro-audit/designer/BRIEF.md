# Design brief: the DEFXN portal screen (FXN ID at the centre)

Reader: the owner's designer, or an image tool. Status: for design directions, 2026-11-09. The current screen is in this folder:
current-ring-rest.png and current-ring-hover.png.

## What this screen is for

This is the first screen of defxn.com after the visitor enters. It shows in one view what an FXN ID can do. An FXN ID is a person's own identity: a key file made on their own device, which no server holds. The visitor should see that their ID is at the centre, and that each part of the network is something their ID can do there.

## The scene

- A large stone portal ring, seen straight on, standing on a dark mirror floor. It is the main focus.
- The ring is made of 12 stones. Each stone carries a small object that shows its topic.
- In the ring's opening floats the FXN ID card: a thin, dark card with chamfered corners, a mint edge strip and a chip. It breathes slowly.
- At rest, a small mint light travels around the ring, the amber slits breathe in a slow wave, and the floor reflects the ring.

## The 12 stones, clockwise from the top

| Stone | What your ID does | What you get back | Status today |
|---|---|---|---|
| FXN ID | Initiate it on your device | A key file that only you hold | Live |
| Records | Sign a record | A receipt that makes it final | Built, not live |
| Keepers | Send your record to the keepers | Copies kept on our own nodes | Built, not live |
| Capacity | Run a machine that keeps records | A share of the fees. Nothing has paid out yet | Built, not live |
| Chains | Start a chain with one signed record | A chain with its own rules | Built, not live |
| Names | Claim a .fxn name | A name that points to your key | Opens after genesis |
| The game | Enter the game with your ID | Your ID, carried into a world | Runs on one Mac today |
| Releases | Sign a release | A version anyone can check | Built, not live |
| Apps | Ship an app as one bundle | An app with its rules inside | The upload door is live |
| Rules | Sign as one of five keys | A rule changes only when four of five sign | Built, not live |
| Deposits | Bring a deposit in | Funds that arrive with their own proof | Built, not live |
| Network | Sign in on a site of the network | One ID for every site that asks | The FXN ID gate is live |

## The interaction

1. At rest: the ring, the card, the 12 emblems and the travelling light. Each stone carries a quiet topic word.
2. Pointing at a stone or its word: a mint light line grows from that stone into the card, and the card turns toward it. A plain panel at the left shows the topic, what your ID does, what you get back and the status.
3. Moving into the centre keeps that link, so the visitor can click there too.
4. Clicking: the stone's emblem travels along the line into the card, the card turns into that topic's object, and a short film of about 3 seconds shows it working. Then the ring returns.

## The problems to solve

- It feels dead and mechanical. Twelve equal things around a ring read like a clock face. It needs hierarchy, rhythm and life.
- The emblems read as grey chips, not as lit objects. Each must say its topic at a glance at about 100 px tall.
- The link from a stone to the card must read as "your ID does this here".
- It must feel premium and intentional.

## Look rules

- Brutal, clean, sharply focused. Matte dark stone, crisp mint light strips, a few amber accents (amber means live or approval).
- No fog, no blur, no glow haze, no glass sparkle.
- FXN Basalt for headlines, FXN Basalt Text for body, IBM Plex Mono for small labels. Sizes 11, 18, 29, 47 and 76 px only.
- One main action per view. It fits 1440x900 and 1280x720 with no scroll. One DEFXN logo at a time.

## Word rules

Plain and true: what it is, what you do, what you get back. Nothing shown as live that is not. Say "Initiate FXN ID".

## What to deliver

The rest state at 1440x900; one hover state; one click transition in 3 or 4 frames; a sheet of all 12 emblems at 100 px and large; notes on motion timing.
