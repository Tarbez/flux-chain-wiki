Layer: app (`js/pages/intro.js` over the archive objects built in fxn-3d). Reader: the owner, then the one building agent. Status: for approval, nothing built yet. Facts come only from `00-facts-*.md` and `08-story-editor-facts.md` ("row N"). Owner rulings of 2026-11-09 win over every specialist. No metaphor appears.

Length: **128 s of film**, plus the visitor's own time at two holds (the portal press, the Share / Don't share press) and the end screen. Four acts, 21 shots. A full object takes one 8 s cell (build 4 s, words 2 s, rest 2 s).

## 1. The story in one sentence

A visitor walks through the portal into their archive, initiates an FXN ID on their device, signs a record that joins the ID's own chain and comes back with a final receipt copied to our nodes, decides Share or Don't share when a site asks, keeps the key when a site asks for it, opens the same file on a second device, and then sees that key start a chain, ship an app and open the gate to the game, with every claim carrying its status.

## 2. What every visitor understands at the end

1. This room is my archive. Records I sign land here, and the network under it keeps them in copies.
2. I initiated my FXN ID on my device. It is a key file, and no server holds it. I name it and seal it with a PIN.
3. Each record I sign joins my own chain. A receipt comes back, and that receipt is final. Nothing is rewritten.
4. When a site asks, I see the exact site and bytes, then I say Share or Don't share. A site gets my signature, never my key.
5. The same key starts a chain, ships an app and opens the gate. Keepers hold the copies. The pay rule has paid nobody yet. This runs on our development network today. Genesis is next.

## 6. Light, motion and type: the rules

- Light is the status. Filled mint: live now. Edge light, strips at .45: built, not live. Dark outline with a mono name: design only. An edge-lit object never fills during the film.
- Amber is the visitor's act: the key, a press, a signature. One amber point per still. A record is a mint run along a rail. A receipt is the same run back, landing as a steady strip that stays.
- Clock 120 BPM. A cell is 4 bars: staged build (parts on rails, lock with small overshoot), words at bar 3, rest at bar 4. Five hits, each followed by a bar of hold: the mark's glow, the tear, the arrival flash, the first receipt, the gate.
- One continuous camera at eye height, cuts only on an object's lock. Close belongs to the key alone. Jitter is 0 inside the archive. Type: FXN Basalt 76 / 47 / 29, IBM Plex Mono 11. Words never fade. A number never stands without its condition. Reduced motion: builds placed at once, the tear a cross-fade, cells keep their length.
