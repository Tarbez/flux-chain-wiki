# Intro film: dramatic structure (series head writer, 2026-11-09)

Layer: app (the site intro over the fxn-3d site objects). Reader: the producer who merges the drafts, then the site session that builds. Status: draft, not confirmed, nothing built. Facts: SPACE.md, the identity manifests, `00-facts-genesis.md`, `00-facts-game.md`, the names doc, the object queue. Tone only from DIRECTION.md and SITE-FILM.md.

## The spine

A visitor walks through the portal into a room that is theirs. A key is made on their device. Records about them start to exist. They decide, one press at a time, who may read them. Then they see what that key will open: chains, apps, worlds, and a network where the people who run machines get paid. One door is left open. The visitor is the protagonist, the objects are the cast, the kernel is the stage.

## Why a story and not a tour

The 60 s draft shows fourteen true objects and none is about the person watching. SITE-FILM.md's 18 scenes with three exits each are a map with no midpoint. A story needs a want (to be someone here), a threat (a server holds your key, you cannot see who reads your records) and a choice (the Share or Don't share press). Every object arrives after that press as its consequence. Target: 75 to 90 s plus the visitor's own time. A hold waits for a press. The world acts first, the line names what was seen, the reason sits behind a "Why?" chip.

## Truth states, one per beat

The new devnet is not deployed and nothing has paid out. Each beat carries one tag, said in its 11 mono line: **LIVE** (old infra: FXN ID gate, name gateway, bundle door), **BUILT, NOT LIVE** (receipts, pins to 3 of 4 named pinners, chain launch, multisig, bridge, pay rules), **MEASURED, PROTOTYPE** (3/6/9/16 keepers = 6,006/15,222/23,014/35,734 final/s, prototype 8fb74f5, not the current build), **LOCAL ONLY** (World Studio, the portal world), **DESIGN** (live rooms, referrals, "a world is a chain"). Act 3 opens with one 11 mono line: "What follows is built and waits for the network to go live." A DESIGN item is a dark outline that never lights.

## Two alternative cold opens

**A. The empty card.** Black. A card slides in, face blank, stops. "Nobody has written on this yet." The click tears the picture and the glitch carries the visitor to the archive, where the same card waits. Hook: a blank object that is theirs. Cost: Portal 101, confirmed as the opening, leaves the first frame.

**B. The watched record.** Through the portal as planned, but the first thing in the dark is one lit record ringed by dim screens turned toward it. "Who is reading this?" The screens go dark as the lights wake. Hook: threat first. Cost: welcome comes first.
