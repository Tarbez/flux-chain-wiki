# Speed facts

For: the people who build the DEFXN site pages. Data file: `js/content/speed-facts.js` (`ArkUI.speedFacts`). As of 2026-11-09.

Times and rates measured on test machines before genesis, with throwaway keys. The new devnet is not deployed, nothing is live and nothing has paid out.

Each row is one measurement. A number with no measurement is not here: it is in the list at the end. Paths starting with docs/ are under /Volumes/PortableSSD/.

## Two values for one thing

- Pin lag: a mean of 1,350 to 1,696 ms on 4 machines (keeper-measure-2026-11-07.md, "4.2 The keeper's own receipt-to-pinned"). A mean of about 1.1 s on 5 nodes on one host (devnet-roadmap.html, "More numbers, and what failed"). The setups differ.
- Swap to Final: 6.0 s on the batch auction (balance-in-record-study.md). Rehearsal 2 read 42 to 44 s per swap, because the driver waited too long (same doc, "Which swap path the 6.0 s is").
