The three causes behind most misses

  • Colour names worked out once at the page root. CSS works out a colour name such as --dx-edge: hsl(var(--border-control)) where it is declared. Declared on :root, it keeps the page's colour, because the skin sets its colours lower down, on #scene. Fixed here for the kit's --dx-edge and --dx-link and for the site's --signal-* names. Still open: the SDK's alias block (--signal, --action-*, --dak-*) in css/flux-themes.css, which is generated from the SDK.
  • The skin sets a colour but not its steps. Card 27 sets --primary and --secondary, but --primary-100 to --primary-900 and --secondary-100 to --secondary-900 keep the site's orange and teal. The fix belongs in the skin's token recipe (NFT session); it was told on 2026-10-25.
  • Panels that set their own colours. The kit's Products panel set every colour name inside itself. Fixed here. No other panel does this today: the audit found no other local remap.

Fixed in this pass

  • Products panel: the kit set its own colour tokens, so a skin stopped at its edge. css/navigation.css now hands it the site's tokens.
  • Products panel: no part had an Ark UI part name, so no skin rule reached it. js/ark/skin-parts.js now names 33 of its pieces.
  • Header: header.ark-header nav named all three navs. The skin's padding then moved the mark over Products and hung the Products panel 765px to the right. Only the page links are named now; the account panel is a popover.
  • Products rows: a skin's icon bullet took the first grid cell and pushed each row's picture and words one column right. Each piece now has its own cell.
  • Bar links and the Products panel: a skin's 24px padding squeezed fixed-size controls. css/skin-site.css keeps their padding.
  • Bar controls: the kit's --dx-edge and --dx-link now follow the skin (css/skin-site.css).
  • Products panel: under a skin it opened about 2 s late, because each dressing step restarted its fade-in. Under a skin the fade-ins are off (css/skin-site.css).
  • Products rail: as an ASIDE part it drew a second frame around the category list and narrowed it on a phone. Only the list is named now.
  • Products panel on a phone: a card's "columns" layout made the panel a flex row, so the category list shrank to 197px of 358px. The panel stays the kit's grid under a skin.
  • Get FXN Browser had no part name. It is now SECONDARY|BUTTON.
