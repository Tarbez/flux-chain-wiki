# Home redesign: 90 fixes (audit 2, 2026-10-23)

Reader: the agent who fixes the mockup, and the owner who audits it after.
Source of the audit: the owner's boards (panels 02, 03, 04, 05), the running app at 1280x720,
1440x900 and 390x844, the mid-flight frames from `motion.mjs`, and the code.
Owner's words: "the chain page animation and the 3D is not good enough", "expand the size",
"the open page background should become bigger".

Each fix names the problem, the fix and the check. Work in order: A and B first.
Mark each one done, skipped or changed in the agent's report. Never invent data.

## A. Chain page: the owner's redesign (panel 02)

1. The ring is sparse: 5 tiles far apart with long links. Pack the 5 record tiles close, so the gap between two tiles is at most 0.4 of a tile. Check: screenshot.
2. The ring is small on screen. Make it fill at least 75% of the stage height at both desktop sizes. Narrow the page to 440px so the ring gets the room.
3. The camera is too flat (17 degrees). Raise it to about 32 degrees, so the ring reads as a ring, as on the boards.
4. Your key is small and sits behind the front tile. Make it about 1.4 tiles wide, raise it above the ring's centre, and give it the teal rim. It faces the camera.
5. Add the board's label at the key: "Your chain" and "5 records", as a 3D tag.
6. The entry has no order. Play it as a sequence: the camera dives, the empty slots fold away, the record tiles rise and turn outward, the chain links join one by one from oldest to newest, the key rises with one ring of light, then the chosen tile lifts and the page opens. Check: frames at 300, 700, 1100 and 1500 ms each show the next step.
7. On entry, one teal bead runs once along the links, oldest to newest. It shows that each record links to the one before it. It stops at the newest record.
8. Mark the chain's two ends with small tags: "Oldest" and "Newest".
9. The chosen tile lifts, grows 15% and gets a soft teal light under it. The other tiles step back 25% darker.
10. Turning the ring uses a per-frame factor, so its speed depends on the frame rate. Use a timed ease of 450 ms.
11. Move the turn arrows from the stage's sides to under the ring, with a "1 of 5" count between them.
12. Hover sync: a row under the pointer lifts its tile; a tile under the pointer marks its row.
13. A hover tag on a tile says what a click does: "Open" on the chosen tile, "Choose" on the others.
14. "Not signed" is too faint to read. Use muted text on a dashed border, at a contrast of at least 4.5:1.
15. Make the page the board's fact card and list: Records, Newest and Signed in the strip, then a "Next in chain" row. Oldest moves to the record page's timeline.
16. Add the board's tabs: Records and Activity. Activity lists the sample's history steps across the chain, newest first. A record with none says so.

## B. Open record: a bigger card (owner)

17. The card is small (780px wide). Make it min(1180px, 88vw) wide and min(74vh, 640px) tall. The title grows to about 40px.
18. The card is a stretched tile, so its corners stretch into ovals. Draw it with a panel shape whose corner radius stays the same in world units.
19. The entry only widens the tile. Bring the tile to the camera, turn it a quarter as it widens, and let it land. Keep it under 1.2 s, and let it settle.
20. The neighbour tiles are large and cut by the screen's edges. Make them smaller, further back and 40% darker. They slide out to the sides as the card widens.
21. Record to Open is two moves: the page folds back into the tile, then the tile widens. Make it one move: the page hands over to the tile halfway and the tile becomes the card.
22. Tabs jump. Slide one pill between the tabs.
23. A tab's content wipes in from the side of the tab you came from.
24. Use the new room: Content in columns, and a facts column on the right (Type, State, Signed by, Next in chain), as on board 04.
25. Put the crumbs path in the card's top line: "Personal chain / Project notes".

## C. Motion across the app

26. Every flight takes 1750 ms. Scale it with the camera's travel, from 1100 to 2300 ms.
27. Long flights pass through the network. Lift the camera in an arc in the middle of a long flight, more for a longer one.
28. The JS easing is not the CSS easing (`--glide`). Use one curve, cubic-bezier(.22,.7,.18,1), in both.
29. The words wait a fixed delay, so the page is empty for a moment. Let the scene say when the page is open (p above 0.85), and start the words then.
30. The page glides flat from page to page. Add a slight turn (6 degrees at most) and lift halfway, then settle flat.
31. The page is plain grey plastic. Make it darker, with a thin light rim, like the boards' dark glass.
32. The page is always 600px tall, so its bottom is empty on most pages. Size it to its words (600px at most); the 3D page follows.
33. The 3D labels all show at the end at once. Show them one after another in story order, 80 ms apart.
34. A 3D label has no line to its point. Add a small square on the point and a 1px leader, as on the home callouts.
35. The network dims late in a flight. Dim it early (40% in), so the stage reads when the camera arrives.
36. A new address in the middle of a flight can leave two views on screen. Cancel the old timers and clean the classes on every route.
37. Check that every new motion follows reduced motion: stage tweens, followers, swaps, the tab pill.
38. Large blocks overshoot as they grow and look rubbery. Keep the overshoot for small items only; large ones glide.
39. Hover lift differs from 0.06 to 0.45. Use one rule: 12% of the object's height.
40. A pointer over a 3D thing gives no name. Show a hover tag with its name and what a click does.
