DEFXN · identity card · wear audit

Why wears feel alike

Measured on the running site at 1440×900 on five pages, in the site's own look and under sample cards 27, 263 and 512. 2026-10-24. Every card is a sample. The same list is in the repository at docs/home-redesign/nft/WEAR-AUDIT.md.

"Most of the elements are not translated into the UI, and I do not feel the experience of changing different wears. The idea is to transform content."

The measurements agree. Six causes:

  1. Wears are alike. Cards 27 and 512 bring the same rules to every page. A third of all cards share one structure, and every card gets the same marker.
  2. Little of the page is reached. 8 to 11% of parts take a rule. The main reading panel, the headings and the footer never change.
  3. What changes is too small to see. Pieces arrive at 72x16 px with 9 px words, in inks of 7.5 to 42% white on near-black.
  4. The art never reaches the site. Background, glow, light and particles have no way onto a page, because the card world shows only where the ring world is off. That is 318 of 831 elements.
  5. Content does not transform. Layout, density, type voice, images, numbers and icons stay the site's in every wear.
  6. Changing a wear has no moment. The switch is a cut with nothing moving, it is reachable from one page, and a reload drops it.

The 100 issues

100 shown

AEvery wear looks alike 12

The rules a card brings barely differ from card to card.

  1. A1

    Cards 27 and 512 dress every page with exactly the same rules.

    Evidence Ark UI's report on 5 pages: same rule counts and values. Card 263 differs only by a cut corner instead of a soft one.

    Fix Derive more rule axes per card (A2 to A6).

  2. A2

    Every card gets the same list marker. Fix first

    Evidence 600 of 600 cards derive marker: emblem, because every icon element draws something.

    Fix Derive the marker from the icon's shape: a count, an icon, or none.

  3. A3

    Three rules are almost constant.

    Evidence Over 600 cards: frame full 74%, ornament strip 87%, header tag 86%.

    Fix Measure more classes from the drawings: double line, dashed, notched, glowing, heavy.

  4. A4

    One structure covers a third of all cards.

    Evidence The top rule set covers 34% of 600 cards. The top two cover 48%.

    Fix Widen the closed sets, and add axes that are derived from other layers.

  5. A5

    No born card ever gets a layout or a density. Fix first

    Evidence 0 of 600 cards wear a whole set, and only a whole set may carry layout and density.

    Fix Derive layout and density for every card from its traits, not only for full sets.

  6. A6

    Only 4 of 12 layers shape the structure.

    Evidence Rules come from frame, ornament, icon and tag. The other eight layers, from palette to divider, shape nothing.

    Fix Map each layer to one axis: background to layout, particles to density, light to motion, corner to edge.

  7. A7

    The edge is the only rule that varies, and it shows as 2 to 4 px.

    Evidence Card 263 against 512: a cut corner instead of a soft one on buttons and panels.

    Fix Give each edge value a scale per part kind, so a cut or a notch reads at a glance.

  8. A8

    A Legendary card and a Common card dress a site the same way.

    Evidence Rules never come from rarity (by design), and nothing else marks rarity on the site.

    Fix Show rarity as content: a rarity badge in the identity zone, never as rules.

  9. A9

    Page backgrounds look the same for every card.

    Evidence Surface tokens keep saturation at 6.6 to 7.2% and lightness at 3.6 to 8.4%: near-black for all.

    Fix Let surfaces take more of the card's hue, inside the contrast gate.

  10. A10

    Text and border colours never change.

    Evidence --text stays 0 0% 80% and --border-default stays white at 16% for every card.

    Fix Tint muted text and borders with the card's hue, held by the contrast gate.

  11. A11

    A worn variant changes only accent colours.

    Evidence A Copper skin on card 263 changes the buttons and links, and no shape.

    Fix A worn element must change what its layer shapes (A6).

  12. A12

    A two-hue palette shows mostly one hue.

    Evidence Buttons take --complement and links take --role. Headings, tabs and rules take no card hue.

    Fix Give the second hue a fixed role: headings' accent, the active tab, the rules.

BMost of the page never changes 15

Wearing reaches a small part of each page.

  1. B1

    Wearing a card changes 0.5 to 2% of the screen.

    Evidence Pixel difference at 1440x900 on /, /about, /palette and /resolutions. /account reaches 5.5% because the profile card shows there.

    Fix Set a floor: a wear must visibly change the page's main surfaces (B4, D7).

  2. B2

    Switching from one card to another changes 0.5 to 2.1% of the screen.

    Evidence Pixel difference between cards 27, 263 and 512 on 5 pages.

    Fix Measure wear distinctness as a test (I5), and raise it with A2 to A6.

  3. B3

    Only 8 to 11% of parts take any rule.

    Evidence Ark UI's report: 7 to 23 parts with rules, out of 89 to 203 parts per page.

    Fix Open more parts in the site's table (B5, B6).

  4. B4

    The page's main panel never changes. Fix first

    Evidence The large reading panel on /about takes no frame, edge or background in any wear.

    Fix Make the page panel a skinned surface: frame, edge and a background pattern (D7).

  5. B5

    Layout and density are off for every part.

    Evidence DefxnSkinParts.ALLOW grants no layout or density. Ark UI's layouts sit unused.

    Fix Turn on reviewed layouts part by part, starting with the mega dropdown and tabs.

  6. B6

    13 of 31 Ark parts take colours only.

    Evidence The gallery: eyebrow, steps, quote, meter, table, breadcrumb, term, field, status line, choice, lane, band, toggle group.

    Fix Give each a role from its part kind: rules for tables, marks for steps, frames for fields.

  7. B7

    The navbar keeps the site's look.

    Evidence It takes frame and edge only. The visible change is one corner cut and the 22 px avatar.

    Fix Give the navbar a card layout, a card frame and the identity zone (see the earlier plan).

  8. B8

    The Products dropdown barely changes.

    Evidence Under card 263: a copper top line, copper dots and one copper link. Its feature cards take no frame.

    Fix Frame the feature cards, mark the groups with the card's icon, and allow its tiles layout.

  9. B9

    "Run DEFXN" keeps the site's teal in every wear.

    Evidence Screenshots of /about under 27, 263 and 512: the button is unchanged.

    Fix Map the kit button to a skinned part, or document why it is locked.

  10. B10

    The footer never changes.

    Evidence Footer bar identical in all four looks.

    Fix Give the footer a divider and the card's small marks.

  11. B11

    Headings keep their type in every wear.

    Evidence h1 to h3: same face, size, weight and case under every card.

    Fix Add a type-voice rule (E3).

  12. B12

    Tabs change only their underline colour.

    Evidence /about tabs under 263 and 512.

    Fix Allow Ark UI's tab layouts: pills, rail, underline.

  13. B13

    Numbers and data keep the site's style.

    Evidence Stats, meters and tables are unchanged on /about and /palette.

    Fix Style numbers in the card's mono voice, with its divider between rows.

  14. B14

    Form fields take colours only.

    Evidence Fields and choices on /account and /palette: no frame, edge or focus shape.

    Fix Give fields the card's edge and a frame on focus.

  15. B15

    Most skin pieces sit in closed menus.

    Evidence 82 icon markers are drawn at 0x0 inside menus that are not open.

    Fix Spend the card's pieces on what is visible first.

CWhat changes is too small or too faint 12

The card's drawings arrive at a size and an ink that disappear.

  1. C1

    The ornament strip is 72x16 px. Fix first

    Evidence Measured on /palette under card 512. Its drawing reads as a dotted line.

    Fix Draw pieces at a UI scale with a minimum size per piece.

  2. C2

    The tag's words are 9 px and unreadable.

    Evidence Measured text height on /about and /account.

    Fix Set a minimum text size for chrome words, or drop words below it.

  3. C3

    Dividers are 104x20 px.

    Evidence Measured on /palette.

    Fix Let a divider run the width of its part.

  4. C4

    Most chrome lines vanish on dark surfaces.

    Evidence Chrome inks are white at 42%, 16% and 7.5% (line, rule, faint) on near-black.

    Fix Use stronger inks for UI chrome, held at 3:1 like a control's edge.

  5. C5

    Fine detail collapses at UI size.

    Evidence Pieces are drawn for a 1000-unit card and shrunk onto parts: dots and hatching merge.

    Fix Give each piece a UI drawing, or a simplified level of detail.

  6. C6

    The avatar is 22 px.

    Evidence The account button's avatar: the card's logo art is not recognisable at that size.

    Fix Use a larger identity zone, or a simplified avatar cut.

  7. C7

    Corners show only when a frame falls back.

    Evidence Corner pieces appear only for frame corners or bracket: 2 parts on /about.

    Fix Give corners their own rule, starting with the page panel and the cards.

  8. C8

    The icon marker is taken back on list rows.

    Evidence Ark UI: "it grows the part's box".

    Fix Reserve the marker's room in the list's own layout when a skin is on.

  9. C9

    Wide frames read as a thin outline.

    Evidence A full frame across 900 px or more: the signature middle shapes stay small.

    Fix Scale the kept bands with the part's size.

  10. C10

    The card's accent is spent on hairlines.

    Evidence Only buttons carry a large area of card colour.

    Fix Spend the accent on one large area per view: the active state or the page panel's edge.

  11. C11

    Nothing on the page says a card is worn.

    Evidence Only the 22 px avatar shows it.

    Fix Show the wearing state in the identity zone: the card's name, set and a way back.

  12. C12

    The tag shows sample words that mean nothing on a site.

    Evidence "SAMPLE" and "PROTOCOL" on a sign-in notice.

    Fix Fill the tag with the part's own label, or the wearer's name.

DCard elements never reach the interface 14

Five of twelve layers have no way onto a site page.

  1. D1

    Backgrounds never show on the site.

    Evidence 89 elements. The card world shows only where the ring world is off.

    Fix Translate each background into a 2D pattern for the page panel (D7).

  2. D2

    Glows never show.

    Evidence 93 elements.

    Fix Translate a glow into the focus halo and the active state's light.

  3. D3

    Lights never show.

    Evidence 55 elements.

    Fix Translate a light into a divider beam and the loading line.

  4. D4

    Particles never show.

    Evidence 48 elements.

    Fix Translate particles into a still texture on surfaces.

  5. D5

    The logo art shows only in the avatar and the profile card.

    Evidence 33 elements.

    Fix Use it as a watermark in the page panel's corner and as the seal.

  6. D6

    38% of the shop has no visible effect on a site.

    Evidence 318 of 831 elements are art layers.

    Fix Give each art layer a 2D translation before it is sold for sites.

  7. D7

    No 2D translation of the art exists. Fix first

    Evidence The site already ships Ark background generators (contours, fabric, lattice, rift, tiles, typefield), unused by skins.

    Fix Map each background element to a generator and its settings, derived from the element, never by hand.

  8. D8

    Corners show only on fallback frames.

    Evidence 77 elements.

    Fix See C7.

  9. D9

    Dividers show only on separators.

    Evidence 52 elements. Few pages have separator parts.

    Fix Draw the divider on every rule line a part already has.

  10. D10

    Tags show on one to three parts per page.

    Evidence 75 elements. Only alert, panel and modal headers take a tag.

    Fix Use the tag as the page title's label and the identity zone's nameplate.

  11. D11

    Icons never become icons.

    Evidence 77 elements. They appear only as list markers.

    Fix Use the icon for bullets, the seal, empty states and the pointer over non-text areas.

  12. D12

    The card's own fields never appear on the site.

    Evidence Name, number, DNA, network and joined date show only on the profile card.

    Fix Show the wearer's fields in the identity zone.

  13. D13

    The card's type voice never reaches the site.

    Evidence The site keeps its three font slots, and the card brings no type rule.

    Fix A type-voice rule inside the three slots (E3).

  14. D14

    The 12 sets have no site expression of their own.

    Evidence A set changes only the same five rules.

    Fix Give each set a signature: a layout, a density and a motion together.

FThe moment of changing a wear 10

Putting on a card does not feel like an event.

  1. F1

    The switch is a cut. Fix first

    Evidence 0 animations run when a wear goes on. It takes 320 to 600 ms with nothing moving.

    Fix One arrival timeline: pieces draw on, colours move, and it ends in under a second.

  2. F2

    The demo's arrival sequence never reached the site.

    Evidence nft-engine.js has a boot step. skin-site.js has none.

    Fix Port the arrival as a site timeline that ends, and skips under reduced motion.

  3. F3

    Fix A wear control in the identity zone, on every page.

  4. F4

    Evidence A visitor cannot try a worn variant or compare cards on the page.

  5. F5

    A reload drops the wear.

    Evidence Nothing is kept.

    Fix Keep the choice in the identity (the earlier plan).

  6. F6

    There is no try-on preview.

    Evidence The only states are on and off.

    Fix Preview on hover or press, then confirm.

  7. F7

    The site does not say what changed.

    Evidence No message after wearing.

    Fix One status line: "Wearing card 263, Copper".

  8. F8

    The site does not greet the wearer.

    Evidence No nameplate, no presence.

    Fix Show the wearer's name and card in the identity zone.

  9. F9

    What a visitor wears does not lead to the shop.

    Evidence No link from a worn piece to its shop item.

  10. F10

    A wear does not carry to other DEFXN sites.

    Evidence Nothing is held by the identity.

HNames do not travel 6

The owner's ask: real interface names that work outside the card.

  1. H1

    Piece names are card words.

    Evidence tag, ornament, emblem, sigil, strip and rule mean little to a site, a game or an app.

    Fix Rename to interface roles (the name map below).

  2. H2

    Art layer names do not say what they do.

    Evidence backdrop, aura, signal and mark.

    Fix background, glow, light and logo.

  3. H3

    One piece has several names.

    Evidence The icon is emblem (slot), Sigil (element) and marker (Ark rule). The strip is ornament and Strip. The divider is divider and Rule.

    Fix One name per piece, everywhere.

  4. H4

    Old names are written into records.

    Evidence The review file, skin text and pins use layer keys.

    Fix Keep old names readable as aliases. Nothing written is rewritten.

  5. H5

    The table version hashes folder and element names.

    Evidence make-layers.mjs tableVersion: a rename would change every card's table version.

    Fix Hash stable ids, kept through the alias table.

  6. H6

    File order sets the DNA.

    Evidence Elements are read in sorted file order, so renaming a file can move it and change DNAs.

    Fix Never rename element files. Rename the vocabulary, not the files.

ITools and process 8

Nothing measured how a wear feels until this audit.

  1. I1

    No test measures how much a wear changes a page.

    Evidence This audit's pixel difference was done by hand.

    Fix A wear test with a floor per page.

  2. I2

    The Skin lab offers only 3 samples.

    Evidence The owner cannot try a born card or a worn variant there.

    Fix Let the lab take any genesis number and any worn variant.

  3. I3

    The review page judges elements on the card only.

    Evidence Elements are never seen on site parts before they are kept.

    Fix Add a site view to the review page.

  4. I4

    The 3 review samples hide the problem.

    Evidence Two of them derive identical rules.

    Fix Pick review samples by rule distinctness, not by number.

  5. I5

    No metric for wear distinctness.

    Evidence No number says how different two cards make a page.

    Fix Measure pairwise pixel difference over a set of cards and pages.

  6. I6

    The cover check runs only at apply and late mount.

    Evidence Popovers and resizes are not re-checked.

    Fix Re-check on open and on resize (Ark UI is fixing it).

  7. I7

    The gallery shows parts at two thirds of their size.

    Evidence primitives.html zooms tiles to fit a group in view.

    Fix Add a full-size view per part, side by side across wears.

  8. I8

    The owner cannot see what a card could add to a part.

    Evidence The Skin lab lists rules, not pictures.

    Fix Show each candidate rule as a picture on the part, to approve or refuse.
