DEFXN identity card · nine loops · 2026-10-24

A card now changes the page, not only its trim.

Written for the owner. Each card brings its own rules, colours, type and motion, all from its own 3D layers and drawings. A wearer can try a card, keep it after a yes, and see it on every page after a reload. You chose the card's own rules for the real site, and they are live: 25 to 84 parts per page now take them.

Measured in headless Chrome at 1440x900 on the gallery (123 Ark UI parts) and on 5 real pages, under sample cards 27, 263 and 512. Every card is a sample. Nothing is live.
