About this site

This site loads nft-engine.js. It names where a visitor may show, and the visitor's card fills those places: the colours, the panel frames and corners, the heading strips, the dividers, the badges, the bullets, the avatar and the banner.

  • A guest sees the site's own look.
  • Every card shows a look of its own.
  • Leaving puts the site's look back.
